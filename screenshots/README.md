- `alarm_overlay.png`: High-contrast Alarm Overlay with Voice synthesis and custom recording.
- `caregiver_monitor.png`: Family Caregiver Remote Adherence Tracker & Nudge system.
- `health_log.png`: Clinical Health Vitals Logger & Emergency Doctor Directory.
- `analytics_report.png`: Weekly Adherence Bar Chart & Printable Doctor Compliance Report.
